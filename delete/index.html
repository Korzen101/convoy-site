Delete your account

Still signed in? Do it in the app: Settings → Account → Delete Account. It happens at once.

Cannot sign in any more? Ask here. We email a six-digit code to the recovery email on the account; enter it below, and the account and everything in it is deleted within 30 days.

What is deleted

Your profile, your garage and its photos, your recorded runs, your posts and comments, your achievements, and the messages you sent, which go from the other person’s copy of the conversation too. Any run you hosted is deleted for everybody who was on it. Routes you drew are kept, with your name removed, so other people’s runs still have a line to follow. It cannot be undone.

No recovery email on the account?

Then no code can reach you. Write to convoy@korzengroup.com with your username, and we will delete the account once we can tell it is yours.
