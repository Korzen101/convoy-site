The short version

Convoy is a social app for car enthusiasts and a set of tools for driving together. Use it lawfully, drive safely, be decent to the people you meet on it. Break that badly enough and your account goes away. Everything below is the longer form of those four sentences.

Who may use it

You must be at least 13 years old, and at least 16 if you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom. If you are under the age where you live at which you can agree to something like this on your own, you need a parent or guardian to agree for you. You also need to be somewhere Convoy is lawful to use.

Driving comes first

Convoy shows live positions, waypoints and push-to-talk while you drive. None of it is a reason to look at, hold or operate a phone at the wheel. Set the run up before you move, mount the phone, and use voice. You are responsible for obeying the speed limits and traffic laws where you are, and nothing in the app — a route, a waypoint, a lap time or an achievement — is an instruction or an encouragement to break them.

Track sessions

Lap timing and best-lap records are for closed circuits and organised events. Timing yourself on a public road is not what the feature is for and is dangerous. Whoever runs the circuit sets the rules there, and those rules come before anything the app says.

Achievements are not a challenge

Badges record what a drive was, after it happened. They are not targets, they are not scored against anyone else, and no badge requires exceeding a limit. Driving dangerously to earn one is a misuse of the app.

Police reports

A convoy can mark that a police vehicle was seen at a place, and the app also draws areas from published police open data. Both are shared with the people on your run so a group driving together knows what the road ahead is like. They mean POLICE ACTIVITY and nothing more — not danger, not an invitation to speed elsewhere, and never a reason to interfere with, follow, obstruct or avoid law enforcement. Using them to evade the police or to help anybody else do so is a misuse of the app and grounds for removing your account. Reports are what people typed; they are often wrong, always out of date, and are not a safety system.

Your account

You need an account to use Convoy. Keep your password to yourself; anything done from your account is treated as done by you. Tell us at the support address on the app’s store listing if you think somebody else has it.

Closing your account

Settings → Account → Delete Account, which removes your profile, garage, runs, posts and messages and cannot be undone. Any run you HOSTED is deleted for everybody who was on it; routes you drew are kept, without your name on them, so other people's runs still work. Signing out on its own leaves everything where it is. If the control will not work for you, write to the support address on the app’s store listing and it will be done for you.

What you post

You keep ownership of your photos, posts, routes and messages. You give Korzen LLC permission to store them and to show them to the people you shared them with, and to the extent needed to run the app — nothing wider than that, and nothing that lets them be sold or used to advertise at anybody. Do not post anything you do not have the right to post.

What you may not do

No harassment, threats, hate or sexual content involving children. Do not impersonate anybody, do not post somebody else's photos, home or number plate without their say-so, and do not use Convoy to organise street racing, takeovers or anything else illegal. Do not scrape it, do not try to reach data that is not yours, and do not automate an account. Blocking somebody is always available and is not a report; both exist.

Zero tolerance

Convoy has no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. Anything that breaks the rules above can be reported: the ••• on a post or a comment, the flag on somebody's profile or in a conversation, or by pressing and holding a message. A person reads every report within 24 hours; content that breaks these terms is removed, and the account that posted it is suspended or closed. Blocking somebody takes effect at once and does not need a report.

Moderation

Posts and comments you make are yours to delete. Korzen LLC can also remove any post or comment and suspend or close any account, and does so for the reasons above. There is no appeal process at this size beyond writing to the support address on the app’s store listing, which will be read by a person.

Voice

Push-to-talk carries whatever your microphone picks up to everybody on the run. Assume passengers can be heard. The host can mute the channel.

Services Convoy relies on

The app is built with Expo and stores its data with Supabase. Voice may be carried by LiveKit when a convoy is too large for phone-to-phone audio. Routes are drawn with the OSRM public routing service and place searches use OpenStreetMap's Nominatim. Maps come from Apple or Google depending on your phone. Each has its own terms, and an outage in any of them is an outage in Convoy.

No warranty

Convoy is provided as it is, with no warranty of any kind. Positions can be late or wrong, voice can drop, a lap can go uncounted, and a route can send you down a road that is closed. Use your own judgement about where you are actually driving.
